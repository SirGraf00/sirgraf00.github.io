Privacy Policy – SirGraf Uploader

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Controller

Till Graf, Switzerland. Contact: mrtillsch@gmail.com

2. Purpose

SirGraf Uploader is a private, single-user tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

3. Data used

With the account owner's consent, the app uses only the TikTok basic profile information (user.info.basic) and the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely and are used only to post the owner's own content. They are never published.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

6. Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security > Manage app permissions), after which stored tokens are deleted.
